Gravity

Off: bodies are density contrasts against the background — compare with Bouguer anomaly. On: ground above the datum is added at background density (2-D terrain, so only approximate).

Observations & regional

Zero on the model is the all-background model: bodies act only through their contrast with the background, so with no bodies the calculation is exactly 0. The regional ties the data's datum to that zero. Use “none” when the data are already anomalies. “Fit” uses a median-type fit, so unexplained peaks barely move it, and it stays put while you model.
